Buying Guide: How to Choose the Best Monitor Arm

1. Weight and Size Capacity
Always check your monitor's weight and VESA pattern before buying. The ErGear and HUANUO both support up to 17.6 lbs per arm and monitors from 13 to 32 inches. If you have a heavy ultrawide (over 20 lbs), you'll need a sturdier arm. Underestimating weight leads to sagging or gas spring failure over time.

2. Mounting Options
Most arms include either a C-clamp or grommet mount. A C-clamp is easier to install on a standard desk edge, but it won't work on desks with thick trim, glass tops, or a front lip. Grommet mounts require a hole in your desk but are more secure. The ErGear includes both; the HUANUO only offers a C-clamp, so measure your desk's edge thickness first.

3. Articulation and Range of Motion
Look for arms that offer tilt, swivel, rotation, and height adjustment. Gas spring arms are smoother than mechanical ones. For dual setups, independent arm articulation is critical—without it, you can't align mismatched monitors or adjust one without affecting the other. The HUANUO excels here.

4. Cable Management
Good cable management keeps your desk tidy and prevents cables from dangling or getting caught. Integrated channels (like on the HUANUO) are superior to clip-on solutions (like the ErGear's). If you have multiple cables, prioritize arms with spacious channels that can handle power bricks or thick display cables.

5. Desk Compatibility
Your desk's material and thickness matter. Clamp-type arms work best on solid wood or metal desks at least 0.5 inches thick. Avoid glass desks entirely—the clamp pressure can shatter them. If your desk has a thick front trim, look for an arm with a large clamp opening or a grommet mount option.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use a monitor arm on a glass desk?
No, it's not recommended. The clamp pressure can crack tempered glass, especially if the desk isn't perfectly flat. Stick to solid wood or metal desks for safety. If you must use a glass desk, consider a freestanding monitor stand instead.

How do I adjust the gas spring tension on the ErGear arm?
Use the included Allen key to turn the tension screw located near the base of the arm. Turn clockwise to increase tension (for heavier monitors) or counterclockwise to decrease it. Start with the monitor mounted, then adjust until the arm holds its position without drifting up or down.

Will a dual monitor arm work with two different-sized monitors?
Yes, but only if both monitors are within the arm's weight and VESA pattern specifications. The HUANUO's independent arms let you adjust each screen separately, so you can mix a 24-inch and a 27-inch monitor, for example. Just ensure the heavier monitor is mounted closer to the pole to reduce wobble.

Do I need a special desk for a monitor arm?
No, but your desk must have a flat edge at least 0.5 inches thick for a C-clamp, or a pre-drilled hole for a grommet mount. Desks with thick trim, rounded edges, or hollow-core construction may not work. Always check the clamp's maximum opening before buying.
