How to Choose the Best Wireless Keyboard

  • Switch type: Mechanical for feel and longevity, membrane/scissor for quiet and slim profiles.
  • Layout: Full-size for number crunchers, TKL/75% for desk space, 60% for portability.
  • Multi-device: If you switch between laptop, tablet, and phone, look for Bluetooth multi-pairing with dedicated switch buttons.
  • Battery: Rechargeable USB-C is convenient; AA/AAA models can last years without charging.
