Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need a powered USB hub?

A: Not always. If you’re only connecting low-power devices (flash drives, mice, keyboards), a bus-powered hub like the ones above works fine. For external hard drives or multiple high-power devices, consider a powered hub.

Q: Will these hubs work with my iPad or Android tablet?

A: Yes, most USB-C hubs are compatible with iPad Pro/Air and Android tablets, though some features (like HDMI output) may be limited depending on the device.

Q: Can I use these hubs with a Thunderbolt 3/4 port?

A: Yes, they are backward compatible. However, they won’t give you Thunderbolt speeds—they’re USB-C hubs, not Thunderbolt docks.
