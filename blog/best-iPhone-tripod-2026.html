Buying Guide: How to Choose the Best iPhone Tripod

Choosing the right iPhone tripod in 2026 means balancing several practical factors. Here’s what to consider:

1. Stability and Build Quality: Look for tripods with rubberized feet and sturdy leg locks. For the GorillaPod, check that the flexible joints are tight out of the box—loose legs from day one are a red flag. Avoid tripods with thin plastic legs that flex under weight; you want something that can support your phone plus a case or lens attachment without tipping over.

2. Phone Compatibility and Mount: Ensure the clamp fits your iPhone model with or without a case. Measure your phone’s width—many clamps only support devices up to 3.5 inches wide, which may exclude larger Pro Max models with bulky cases. A spring-loaded clamp with rubber padding is ideal to avoid scratches.

3. Portability vs. Versatility: A flexible tripod like the GorillaPod is great for travel because it folds small, but it sacrifices stability on flat surfaces. If you mostly shoot indoors on a table, a rigid mini tripod might be better. Think about where you’ll use it most: hiking, desk work, or studio shoots.

4. Additional Features: A Bluetooth shutter remote is a huge plus for group photos or self-portraits—without it, you’ll have to use a timer or tap the screen, which can cause shake. Also consider whether the tripod has a standard 1/4-inch tripod mount for attaching to other gear, like a gimbal or external microphone.

5. Price and Warranty: Don’t overspend on features you won’t use. The GorillaPod at $24.95 is a sweet spot for most users. Check the warranty—many budget tripods offer only 30 days, while better brands provide a year. Read user reviews about long-term durability, especially for flexible legs that can wear out.

Does the GorillaPod work well for video recording?
It works for static shots, but the flexible legs can shift slightly if you bump the tripod. For stable video, ensure the legs are tightly wrapped around a sturdy surface. It’s not ideal for panning or tilting smoothly without a dedicated video head.

How do I prevent the legs from loosening over time?

Is the GorillaPod compatible with other smartphone brands?
Yes, as long as the phone width falls within the 2.3-3.6 inch range. It works with most Android phones too, but the clamp is optimized for iPhones with its spring tension—very wide or thick phones may not secure properly.
