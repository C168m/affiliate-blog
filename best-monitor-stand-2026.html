Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I use these stands on a glass desk?

A: Generally, yes, but you need to be careful. All three stands come with a clamp base that can put pressure on glass. I recommend using a thick piece of wood or a rubber pad under the clamp to distribute the weight. Avoid glass desks with thin tops (under 1/2 inch).

Q: Will these stands work with a curved monitor?

A: Yes, all three can handle curved monitors. The Ergotron HX is specifically designed for heavy curved ultrawides. The HUANUO and Amazon Basics work well with standard curved screens (up to 32 inches).

Q: How do I install a monitor arm?

A: Installation is usually straightforward. You attach the clamp to your desk, mount the VESA plate to your monitor, and then connect the arm. Each product comes with clear instructions. The Amazon Basics is the easiest to install; the Ergotron HX takes a bit more time due to its heavy-duty design.

Q: Do I need a monitor arm if my monitor already has a stand?

A: A monitor arm offers several advantages: it frees up desk space, allows for better ergonomics (adjustable height and tilt), and reduces neck and eye strain. Most built-in stands are limited in adjustability. If you spend more than 4 hours a day at your desk, an arm is a worthwhile upgrade.

Q: Can I use these arms for a sit-stand desk?

A: Absolutely. All three arms are compatible with standing desks. The gas springs maintain their tension regardless of desk height, so your monitor stays at the same relative position.
