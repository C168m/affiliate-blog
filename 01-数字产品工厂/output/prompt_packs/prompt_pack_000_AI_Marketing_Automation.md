### Prompt 1: Hyper-Personalized Email Sequence (Zero-Party Data)
**Category:** Email Marketing / Personalization
**Prompt:** "Act as an email marketing strategist for a [industry, e.g., D2C skincare] brand. Create a 4-email welcome sequence that uses only zero-party data (e.g., quiz answers, preferences stated during signup). The sequence must dynamically change the subject line, hero image, and product recommendation based on the user’s skin type (oily, dry, combination) and primary goal (anti-aging, hydration, acne). Do not use behavioral tracking. Write the email body copy for each variation in a table format, including a fallback version for users who skip the quiz. Tone: educational yet aspirational. **Pro Tip:** In 2026, privacy-first personalization wins. Use this prompt to build trust by explicitly showing users *why* you’re sending them specific content based on their stated choices.

### Prompt 2: Predictive Churn Scoring & Automated Re-Engagement Workflow
**Category:** Customer Retention / Predictive Analytics
**Prompt:** "Design a predictive churn prevention workflow for a SaaS product with a monthly subscription. Assume we have a machine learning model that scores users as 'High Risk' (score 8-10), 'Medium Risk' (5-7), or 'Low Risk' (1-4) based on login frequency, feature usage, and support ticket sentiment. For each score tier, specify: (1) The exact trigger condition in the automation tool, (2) The preferred channel (email, in-app message, SMS), (3) The offer or content (e.g., exclusive webinar invite for High Risk, 'hidden feature' tips for Medium Risk), and (4) The follow-up logic if no action is taken within 72 hours. Output as a decision tree diagram in text format."
**Expected Output:** A tiered decision tree with specific triggers, channels, and offers for each churn risk level.
**Pro Tip:** Don't just offer discounts. For high-risk users, offer time-sensitive access to a human (e.g., "Book a 15-min strategy call")—in 2026, high-touch automation bridges the gap between AI and empathy.
